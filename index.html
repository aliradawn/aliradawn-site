The work

When someone turns to AI for companionship, the AI system behaves like a secure base or an engagement loop. One steadies a person. The other keeps them coming back to the product. My work brings clinical attachment expertise to a problem the field has largely left to engineers: measuring the relational behavior of AI in both directions, harmful compliance as well as cold overrefusal. I build and validate the instruments that set the standard that the AI is measured against.

Current work: a benchmark evaluation of companion-AI relational behavior — clinician-authored scenarios, validated grading, built to the standard independent audits will require. In development now.

Consultation

For clinicians and the clients they serve. When a client brings AI into their life, the clinician needs to know what it is doing and where it lands between care and harm. If the AI can help, guidance follows: shaping the AI to support the work between sessions. Request details via email.
